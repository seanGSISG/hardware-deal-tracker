## Market-Wide Observations

### Scam Warning
- **HIGH scam risk** for RTX PRO 6000 at prices below $7,000. Multiple confirmed scams at $5,999.
- eBay accounts with long history are being compromised for high-value GPU scams.
- "Classified Ad" listings on eBay are a red flag - these bypass eBay protections.
- Always verify seller feedback count and recent activity patterns.

### Best Value Proposition (May 2025)
1. **RTX 6000 Ada 48GB (Used)** - Best price/performance for 48GB VRAM at ~$4,500-$5,000
2. **RTX PRO 4000 Blackwell SFF** - Good for SFF builds at ~$1,600, 24GB GDDR7
3. **RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell 96GB** - Only if you truly need 96GB, wait for $6,500+

### Pricing Velocity
- RTX PRO 6000: Dropping ~10-15% per quarter (high velocity downward)
- RTX 6000 Ada: Stable, slight downward pressure from new generation
- RTX PRO 4000 SFF: Stable, limited price movement expected short-term

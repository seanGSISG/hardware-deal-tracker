## Market Context

The enterprise U.2 NVMe SSD secondary market remains active with significant price variation depending on seller location (China vs US), drive health/condition, and form factor availability. Key observations:

- **China-based sellers** (Shenzhen/Hong Kong) consistently offer 20-40% lower prices than US-based sellers, with free shipping but longer delivery times (2-4 weeks).
- **US-based sellers** command premiums of $50-200+ but offer faster domestic shipping and easier returns.
- **Health status** significantly affects pricing: drives advertised with 90%+ health sell at full price; drives with 50-79% health trade at 30-50% discounts.
- **Form factor differences matter**: E1.S variants of the Micron 7450 trade at meaningful discounts vs U.2/U.3 variants.
- **Price trend**: Generally **stable to slightly declining** over the past 90 days as more datacenter decommissioning inventory enters the market.

## General Market Analysis: Enterprise U.2 NVMe SSDs (June 2025)

### Key Trends
1. **PCIe 4.0 drives command premiums**: PM9A3 and P5510/P5520 series trade at 2-3x the price of equivalent-capacity PCIe 3.0 drives (P4510, PM983).
2. **Health status is the #1 price driver**: Drives with 90%+ health sell at full market price; drives with 50-79% health trade at 30-50% discounts.
3. **China sellers = lower prices**: Shenzhen/Hong Kong sellers consistently offer 20-40% lower prices, with free shipping but 2-4 week delivery.
4. **Volume discounts available**: Many sellers offer 2-5% discounts for qty 3+, and most accept best offers at 10-20% below list.
5. **Supply is increasing**: More datacenter decommissioning inventory is entering the market, keeping prices stable to slightly declining.
